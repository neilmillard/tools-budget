# Content Plan

Future content roadmap and article ideas for Helpful Money.

## Instagram: disclosed AI persona (DEL-584)

Decision: launch **one** AI-disclosed persona — **"Nan Pearl"** — rather than
the male+female pairing originally floated. helpfulmoney.site currently has
no real Instagram presence or audience (GA4: ~100 sessions/month, almost all
Direct, zero organic, zero conversions), so this is a cold-start channel
test, not an established-audience extension. Validate with one persona
before committing to a second.

- **Persona:** a grandmother character — frugal out of necessity when young,
  debt-free and calm about money now. Visual world is kitchen-table/biscuit-tin/
  allotment, not aspirational luxury. Voice is short, plain-spoken, grandmotherly
  UK English — the "ancient wisdom" half of the site's "Ancient wisdom. Modern
  tools." line, with helpfulmoney.site's guides/calculators as the "modern tools."
- **Disclosure (mandatory):** bio states she's an AI-created character, not
  financial advice. Repeated in the intro post and in captions around any
  follower-growth spike.
- **Content boundary:** budgeting method/habits and general education only
  (zero-based/envelope budgeting, saving habits, myth-busting). No personalised
  recommendations, no investment calls — stays inside the FCA guardrail noted
  on the parent idea (DEL-579).
- **Branding:** posts from Nan Pearl's own account, bio-linked to
  helpfulmoney.site — not posted as the site's own handle. The site has no IG
  equity yet to protect, and character-driven content is what performs in this
  format.
- **First batch (6 posts):** intro/disclosure post; "give every pound a job"
  (envelope budgeting via a biscuit-tin metaphor, linking to the YNAB-style
  guide); "pay yourself first"; myth-bust ("you need a big salary to save");
  a recurring "Nan's rules" one-liner series; a CTA post into the budgeting
  guide on helpfulmoney.site.
- **Dependency:** the CTA post needs Stripe/Brevo set up on helpfulmoney.site
  (tracked in DEL-579) to be useful as a conversion path.

Full writeup: DEL-584.
